About Us

Founded in 2005, Spice Garden began as a small family kitchen with a big dream — to bring authentic Indian flavours to every table. Over the years, we have grown into a beloved neighbourhood restaurant, yet our recipes remain rooted in tradition. Every dish is prepared fresh daily using hand-picked spices, locally sourced vegetables, and a whole lot of love.
